# Initial billing scope

Minimum-spend commitments and end-of-period true-ups are deleted. Periodic and
threshold invoice closes bill the actual rated receivable; they do not invent a
charge to meet a contractual floor. Metered usage, recurring billing, negotiated
rates and allowances, invoice profiles and ordinary collection remain available.

There is no planned SaaS replacement or compatibility module. Reintroducing
minimum-spend commitments would require a separate, explicitly requested product
decision. The fresh schema omits customer_minimum_spend entirely.
